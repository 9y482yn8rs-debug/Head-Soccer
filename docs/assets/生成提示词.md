Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: finished background for a children's 2D side-view Pygame soccer game. Create a beautiful bright cartoon stadium background, landscape 16:9 1280x720 composition. Cheerful sky blue, soft clouds, distant city silhouettes, curved grandstands full of small colorful abstract crowd dots, dark navy outlines and polished cel shading, emerald and lime grass. Fixed side-view arena: back grass begins around 65% down image, field surface extends to bottom, continuous horizontal play ground line at 86% image height. Decorative perspective field stripes very subtle. Keep central lower area calm and open for characters and ball. Match cute big-head football illustration style, professional cohesive game art. No people in foreground, no soccer balls, no goals, no UI, no words, no logos, no watermark. Upper 15% stays low-detail sky for UI overlay.

## 红队动作条

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a single horizontal 5-cell sprite sheet for a children's soccer game. Transparent background with real alpha, no ground, no shadows, no text, no border. Canvas wide 5:1 if possible. Exactly FIVE full-body poses in five equal spaced cells in one row, no overlap, generous clear margins. Each cell has the SAME adorable chibi boy soccer player, huge head half height, fluffy spiky dark brown hair, large expressive brown eyes, rosy cheeks, red short-sleeved soccer shirt with white trim, dark navy shorts, white socks, black football shoes. Friendly determined expression. Thick dark navy outlines, polished colorful 2D cartoon game illustration, simple clean cel shading. Character faces RIGHT in three-quarter side view in all five cells. Left to right: 1 standing ready, 2 running left foot forward, 3 running right foot forward, 4 jumping with knees bent, 5 kicking with right leg extending forward. All figures IDENTICAL scale and identity, head approximately same height, soles aligned on same baseline with comfortable bottom margin; no soccer balls or separate props. This is production game sprite artwork, not a screenshot.
